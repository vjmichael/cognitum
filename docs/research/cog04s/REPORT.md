# COG-04S — separable multidimensional continuum Ritz approximation

Candidate, research only. On normalized unit rectangle/box, tensor product mass M=⊗M_i, stiffness S=sum_i L_i^-2 (⊗ transverse M_j ⊗ S_i), in last-axis-fastest order. Physical lengths rational in [1/2,4]; normalized coordinate pullback with common volume factor canceled. Axis operators are bounded R affine diffusion/potential/weight with independently chosen homogeneous D/N/positive Robin conditions. The weighted multidimensional differential operator is the sum of the scaled one-dimensional weighted operators. Physical Robin flux and potential coefficients transform with length; input coefficients are normalized-coordinate parameters, not arbitrary physical coefficients. No nonseparable coefficients, arbitrary geometry or cross derivatives. Exact weak forms stored as rational Kronecker factors, exact entry evaluation, axis LDL witnesses imply global mass SPD/stiffness PSD (one constant null mode iff all axes have it). No materialization of dense global matrices by candidate. Generalized product Ritz vectors, tensor contractions verify scaled residual and mass orthogonality. First k axis eigenvalues suffice for first k global eigenvalues; repeated eigenvalues preserved within returned window, not full degenerate eigenspace. No claim of rigorous floating-point continuum enclosures.

Five fixtures: isotropic DD square/cube; variable-coefficient mixed rectangle; anisotropic mixed box; all-Neumann cube. Four nested dyadic meshes (2,4,8,16 cells/axis), 3 modes, max 7,000 tensor dof; 19^3=6,859 for all-Neumann. Independent continuum axis Prüfer shooting at two tolerances and analytic constant-coefficient checks; independent numerical quadrature/whitening of every factor, Cartesian sum of complete discrete axis spectra, and direct dense global eigensolver for bounded small systems <=160 dof. Same-head R evidence and exact pins, 10 tests per target, 20 level comparisons and five fresh-process CPU/wall/peak working-set or RSS profiles. Final error threshold abs(error)/max(1,abs(reference)) <1e-5. No claim of speedup over general PDE methods; no separate memory optimization of existing runtime, dependencies or accepted kernels authorized. Native acceptance pending.
