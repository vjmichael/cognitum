Three-seed Gaussian mean/covariance and missing-observation reference. EnKF limited to linear model; nonlinear particle filtering, ensemble localization/inflation and general convergence remain open.

Mode prior applies to first transition, common observation model. Maximum 4096 paths, 2–4 modes; independent scalar regime enumeration, missing sample, label permutation, budget refusal. Exponential scaling; IMM/pruning, switching smoothing/EM and parameter fitting remain open.
