## Mathematical operations and guarantee boundaries

- Exact controllability and observability matrices/ranks for bounded rational finite LTI, continuous and discrete, using the original rational matrix algebra. Exact PBH rank at supplied real or complex-rational spectral point via real block embedding (not an enumeration of every eigenvalue). Reachable subspace basis via independent columns and canonical complement; exact invariant block and quotient. Stabilizability iff unreachable quotient strictly stable; detectability by duality. Full rank alone does not certify identifiability of unknown coefficients or physical data provenance.
- Discrete finite-horizon reachability: solve A^k x0 + [A^(k-1)B,...,B]u = target over rationals, check explicit rollout. Return one unconstrained sparse representative; distinguish its fit to external coefficient/denominator cap, not constrained feasibility or optimal action. No authorization or actuator command. Exact known-input state reconstruction subtracts forced response, checks full stacked observation equations and refuses a unique initial state claim for rank-deficient or inconsistent data.
- Exact Lyapunov equations with rational operator solve: discrete P-A^T P A=W; continuous -(A^T P+P A)=W. Strict stability computed with W=I, exact residual and positive-definite Schur pivots. If operator singular or unique P not PD, strict stability fails (does not distinguish all marginal and unstable cases); arithmetic budget refusal is distinct. Certificate binds model digest, P, residual difference, positive pivots and autonomous sublevel invariance; replay verifies the exact canonical bytes. For supplied rational positive-definite P and rate, exact nonstrict quadratic contraction inequality; continuous A^T P+P A<=-2 rate P, discrete A^T P A<=rate^2 P. These are for autonomous exact declared linear models, not model uncertainty or nonlinear dynamics.
- Exact finite discrete controllability/observability Gramians, exact infinite continuous/discrete Gramians for strictly stable models using the correct dual Lyapunov equations. PSD checks including rank-deficient cases. Exact H2 norm squared for stable state realization (discrete includes D term; continuous requires D=0). No H-infinity norm certification or finite continuous Gramian calculation.
- Numerical complex Schur decomposition with reconstruction/orthogonality residual <=2e-12, sampled induced 2-norm transient growth, including stable defective/nonnormal systems. Not verified eigenvalue enclosures, a global growth maximum or Jordan canonical structure. Continuous exponential norm*horizon <=32; finite bounded gains. Exact discrete interval box overapproximation through signed matrix endpoint propagation; correlation loss can enlarge boxes, not exact reachable sets or nonlinear verified integration.
- Conventional SciPy stabilizing CARE/DARE: rational Q PSD and R PD cost, limited coefficients; finite symmetric/PSD diagnostic and scaled equation residual <=2e-10. Reinterpret returned binary64 feedback gain exactly as rational, compute A-BK, independently solve/check strict rational Lyapunov witness; proves stability of that exact gain for supplied exact model, not physical implementation, certified optimality or exact algebraic root. Reject nonstabilizing or numerically unsupported results; exact arithmetic budget failure propagates. No general LQG/MPC, actuator authority or control execution.
- Scope: existing SSM-01 Model dimensions 1–8, input/output labels not dimensional analysis; horizon 1–64, 4096 numerator/denominator bit limit and original 262144-byte certificate limit.
