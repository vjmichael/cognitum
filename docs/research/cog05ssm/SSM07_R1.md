## Implemented
- DS51: fit the LegS structured bilinear step and real output weights by bounded least squares, fixed continuous generator. Two genuine synthetic parameter-recovery trials, independent held-out sequences. No general trainable DPLR factor or full S4 stack.
- DS53: forward-mode associative affine scan Jacobian-vector product via differentiated composition; real and mixed-complex trajectories against serial tangent recurrence and central finite differences, lengths 1–128. Corrected inherited affine_scan to promote mixed real/complex arrays before composition; corrected the new JVP likewise. Initial dedicated run 26 pass/3 fail due to dropped imaginary components, then 29/29 passed with original tests and tolerances. Reverse-mode, full S5 training and accelerator kernels open.
- DS54: bounded single-channel Mamba-1-style selective recurrence with stable negative exponential rates, causal convolution and SiLU, input-dependent step and B/C, exponential-Euler input term delta*B*u, optional skip and SiLU gate. Serial, scan and separate explicit recurrence agree; future perturbation test. Fit a reduced one-state profile (fixed B, bias; learned log rate, step slope, readout), three independent seed trials; analytic JVP-based Jacobian and central-difference verification. Full multichannel architecture and general parameter training open.
- DS55: scalar-transition SSD reference with matrix-valued state, token-dependent B/C, lower triangular semiseparable mask; independent recurrence, dense matrix and chunked computation with carried state, including zero decay and nonzero initial state. Five block sizes and causality. No optimized accelerator kernel, full Mamba-2 stack or gradients for SSD.
- DS56: stable or neutrally rotating complex diagonal exponential-trapezoid step, convex endpoint mix, time-varying MIMO B/C, previous input contribution as explicit carried state. Independent real two-dimensional matrix exponential equivalence, chunk continuation and second-order convergence in smooth scalar equal-weight fixture. This is a mathematical reference inspired by Mamba-3, not its full parameterization or implementation. No RoPE optimization, general input-dependent rates, DeltaNet/Householder or end-to-end architecture comparison.
- Comparative parity experiment: fixed handcrafted rotation feature, positive-decay feature and last-token feature, each with fitted affine readout, same train/test sequences per seed. Train 128 length-12 sequences; test 128 independent length-24 sequences per seed (1,21,71). Accuracy rotation 100/100/100%, decay 54.6875/39.84375/46.09375%, last token 50/55.46875/46.09375%. This deliberately isolates a known parity representation: rotation is NOT learned end-to-end; no claim of architecture superiority, no GRU or general recall benchmark.
