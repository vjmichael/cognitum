Explicit insufficient interval warmup, empirical coverage and no general coverage guarantee. Deterministic trend/season recovery, past-only leakage control, invalid split refusal. Reference mean/variance estimated separately, finite-sample rank threshold with strict exceedance, insufficient calibration refusal, first alarm index. Under exchangeability conditional on reference, marginal false-alarm level per block only; not a repeated-monitoring or dependent-series guarantee.
