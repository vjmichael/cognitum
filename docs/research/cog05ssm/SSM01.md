Reset/continuation is explicit, no implicit retention or time lookahead.

## Continuous transform

For a constant input on [t,t+h], the upper blocks of exp(h [[A,B],[0,0]]) yield A_d and B_d. This covers singular A without computing A inverse. C and D unchanged for sampled instantaneous pre-update outputs. The requested positive rational h is explicit, independent of nominal step metadata. SciPy expm float64, with a separate scaled Taylor series of 32 terms, block infinity norm <=8, scaling to <=0.5 before squaring. Relative infinity norm disagreement <=2e-12 and finite outputs required. The Taylor cross-check is a numerical diagnostic, not a proved bound on rounding or continuous solutions; output marked uncertified, not converted to an exact rational model.
