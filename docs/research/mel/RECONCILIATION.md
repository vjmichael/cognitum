## Executive conclusion

The master register is an expanded research scope, not an implementation receipt. The current development tree contains a sizable HDC kernel and learning/factorization program, three accepted VFA stages, a much more limited portable KAN inference implementation, and bounded conventional shared mathematical specialists. No full joint HDC–KAN engine exists yet. Native acceptance is scoped to tested behavior, not generality, production admission, physical hardware, or proof of general intelligence.

**Audit category definitions:** B = bounded verified slice of the named group (never blanket completion); P = partial source/experiment coverage; A = adjacent conventional specialist or prior historical evidence only, not current engine coverage; O = open/not independently evidenced in the audited scope. This is a conservative group-level judgment, not a completion percentage. A group may contain dozens of subtasks. Historical algorithm-status fields in the 85-entry research register are not a substitute for its later per-stage evidence and CI receipts.

No edits were made to the repository, original Library register, or implementation as part of this review. Recommendations are pending owner authorization. S01–S24 are audit-assigned names for the original 24 unnumbered shared instruments. Master H12 means fundamental encoding; older research register H12 means acceleration/fault-tolerant devices. Keep these namespaces distinct.

## Mathematical qualification standard

Each scoped operation requires a typed input/output domain, basis/representation and dimensionality, explicit mathematical law or assumption (including conditions for closure), exact/approximation semantics, reference/oracle, numerical error bounds and uncertainty where feasible, conditioning and failure/refusal behavior, matched conventional comparisons, resource and scaling measurements, source/version provenance, and independent acceptance. A result can be supported, experimental, unsupported, or an explicitly negative research result. A general-purpose engine is a growing collection of justified operations, not a theorem that all imaginable mathematics can be low-cost or expressible in one fixed-dimensional hypervector or KAN graph.

**Important boundaries:** RKHS/FPE errors currently use float64 or conditional probabilities, not guaranteed interval certificates. Typed Gaussian coefficient arithmetic supplements genuine random hypervectors; not all operations are direct hypervector binding. Multivariate derivatives of approximations do not automatically bound target derivatives. Converged resonators can be wrong; canonical witnesses remain required. Accurate learned KAN functions do not prove equations, causal effects or safe control laws. Previous stopping-distance and wind/drag/lag research recorded real failure modes; preserve them. Neither HDC nor KAN is production enabled on this head. The complete audit does not authorize changes to Lu, NVP, CapsNet, source branches, CI or production routing.
