## RefineHD caveat

Primary source: https://ics.uci.edu/~givargis/pubs/C80.pdf, section 5.2 algorithm 3. The prose equations use the true/predicted score terms, algorithm uses top two overall; correctly classified update is ambiguous (prose add versus printed multiplicative assignment). Preserve signed cosine and negative top-two multiplier, explicit cold-start, running mean of erroneous winner similarity. Both additive and literal alternatives are tested; poor accuracy (digits 8.54/17.98%, wine 30.23/31.98%) is a failed fidelity/performance inquiry, not reproduced paper result. Still required: author implementation or clarified equations, Flocet control, exact paper datasets and replicated protocols. Do not tune on test.
