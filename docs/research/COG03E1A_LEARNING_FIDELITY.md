Batch of one for sequential online semantics; no claim of calibrated probabilities or full implementation of original variable-learning-rate AdaptHD. Separate native encoders for vanilla/AdaptHD (keys/levels), Online/Neural (sinusoid), DistHD (projection), IntRVFL (density).

Observed in pinned NeuralHD and DistHD: advanced-index view operations `weight.data[:, regen_dims].zero_()`, `encoder.weight.data[regen_dims, :].normal_()` (and NeuralHD bias operation) mutate temporary tensor, not stored source. Real native fit of 64x8 fixture and all eight real-data configs shows zero encoder weight rows changed after requested regeneration. The quick native synthetic failure is recorded, not counted as a successful fit. Independent reference returns D-dimensional zeros, computes DistHD score by normalized prototype distances for partial/complete errors, matches actual native untied score tests, selects stable ties, resets selected prototype columns and explicitly assigns selected projection rows/biases. This implements intended independent corrective reference; not patched upstream, not claim every paper method identical. Native fit remains a control even with defect. No accuracy gain from corrected regeneration in the pilot. NeuralHD final distinct changed rows 13 or 52 (some selected repeatedly); DistHD roughly 50–52 or 202–208. More learning and drift work remains.

IntRVFL: actual native density readout, variance-scaled diagonal regularizer (not ordinary alpha I), independent float64 accumulation and pseudoinverse. Controlled small float64 agreement, near rank-deficient control shows PyTorch default relative cutoff D*eps(dtype). Explicit native_rank and full_rank policies; latter default 1e-15; no bitwise numerical claim. Across 1952 paired prediction events, full-rank reference differs 36, native-cutoff reference differs 1. Native models remain independently executed. No equivalence claim at all conditions.
