## What changes technically

- GHRR is an additional VSA family, distinct from current 13: block/matrix noncommutative multiplication intrinsically preserves order, unlike commutative FHRR. Contrast ordered FHRR, VTB, MBAT; verify left/right inverse and scalar special case. November 2026 work flattens similarity/training/inference, not arbitrary composition, so compare exactly to matrix reference. Add after the original COG-03B native eight-family gate as B2, before final mathematical coverage closure.
- Distinguish learning geometry from symbolic decoding. Correlated encodings can aid generalization, while separated encodings can favor retrieval. Capacity theory uses task-specific failure probabilities, not fixed dimension dogma. Use Hadamard, Nyström PSD kernels, dynamic codebooks, spatial encoding and trainable encoders as distinct comparison arms.
- Recent ARC-VSA paper reports 3.0% on ARC-AGI-1-Eval (10.8% Train) versus 94.5% Sort-of-ARC and 83.1% 1D-ARC; simplified tasks do not establish general intelligence. PathHD preprint reports one model call plus graph path scoring; test with independent path evidence and frozen model in COG-10. Compare genuine explicit symbolic search under equal compute. New cleanup rules make resonator not one algorithm.
- Extend learning beyond classification: native binary/integer encoder training, backpropagation, phase-grid and margin update rules; regression (algebraic vs conventional solver after encoding), clustering, graph learning, open-world anomalies, uncertainty sets, continual, temporal and simulated federated/RL. hdlib 2.1.0 is a second maintained research backend, MIT, Python >=3.11, 62.8 kB universal wheel Sep 25. No actual wheel installation. Preserve without broadening Lu network scope. C17 remains deferred; LDN evaluated only as a standalone reference.
- SupHDC preprint reports Raspberry Pi 5 wall-clock gain 2.01x with mean accuracy loss 2.26 percentage points, not a free gain. Ordinary SIMD/batch encoding must be a strong comparison. ApproxHDC explores automated approximation, not permission to silently weaken verifiers. HDDB/HyperSPACE, RISC-V, QHDC and Quark extend device research; exact software and small simulation only here; physically measured ARM/FPGA remain COG-08/09. Do not interpret QPU circuits or theoretical Grover search as a proven Cognitum advantage.
