## L24: state-space probability and order

Source: Slot et al., Neural Computation 38(8):1469–1489, DOI 10.1162/NECO.a.1544. Public abstract available at https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42330488/ ; publisher full text returned 403. Do NOT claim fidelity to the authors' bitwise update/decay-prevention algorithm. Implement independent categorical HMM fitted by scaled EM with smoothed initial/transition/emission distributions (hidden state labels not used). Exact forward likelihood is evaluated by streaming normalized probability; finite path enumeration is an independent short-sequence oracle. Stochastic binary population hypervectors represent mutually exclusive hidden-state occupancy across packed lanes; transition masks AND the origin mask, OR disjoint contributions, emission masks AND survivors, count then sample from normalized counts. Explicit depletion, no silently invented likelihood. This is stochastic approximate forward with numerical RNG, count and resampling, not an all-bitwise HDC paper reproduction. Bounded lane storage; temporary random arrays/allocator overhead not included in retained-state figures. Binary bigrams: role permutation, XOR and bundle; continuous cosine or separately packed sign-majority/Hamming prototype. Compare exact HMM, conventional bigram logistic and GRU. Training and held-out split shared across arms. Synthetic classes share emission distributions but differ in forward/reverse/totally mixed hidden transitions. Train 72 sequences per seed (24 each), test 30 per condition, lengths 24/72 and insert/delete/repeat perturbations. Three seeds 1301/1307/1319. At 2048 lanes, clean rank-1 agreement with fitted exact HMM 97.8%; no depletion, mean absolute normalized log-score error .0073. Longer clean generalization agreement 96.7%. 128 lanes produce depletion. No CPU speed benefit; exact baseline retained. Conventional bigram strongest under edits, HDC cosine clean slightly better on this small panel; no significance claim.
