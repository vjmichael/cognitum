# COG-04H — verified bounded polynomial differential equations

Parent publication: `9f735116db942e98bc7fc8c5e3bacd9fce1c149a` (E/F); requires the preserved COG-04G overlay and its same-head scientific-operator gate. Research-only, CPU-first. No production promotion, new route, worker authority, or model admission.

## Implemented

- Scalar linear ordinary and partial differential equations with rational polynomial coefficients and source, up to three Cartesian dimensions, derivative order four, represented trial total degree at most twelve and no more than 64 unknown coefficients. Reuses COG-04G differential operators and COG-04F polynomial fields. Bounded exact coefficient matching and rational elimination distinguish unique-in-trial-space, inconsistent-in-trial-space, and underdetermined-in-trial-space; only the first returns a solution. An independent forward-elimination rank check plus original-row dot-product and coefficient-level residual verification reject a false solver result.
- Boundary and initial conditions are entire exact polynomial coordinate-derivative traces on rational box faces. Dirichlet, coordinate Neumann, higher derivatives and mixed constraint sets are supported; outward normal signs are explicit caller conventions. A declared time axis requires initial traces through one less than its largest operator derivative order. Missing/contradictory data, out-of-domain fields and extra fixed-axis dependence are refused.
- `assess_candidate`: exact continuous equation residual, conservative absolute upper bound, exact squared L2 volume integral and exact face squared L2 (point square in 1D), all bound to problem and candidate identities. Independent monomial derivative and trace oracles; replay rejects modified certificates. Nonzero residual reports are diagnostic only, never solution acceptance or a bound on solution error. `solve`, `certify_candidate` and `certify_checkpoint` require exactly zero equation and all declared trace residuals. `certify_checkpoint` uses the existing lossless extraction/replay API and binds the specific checkpoint to the problem and residual report; it does not approve/register or train a model.
