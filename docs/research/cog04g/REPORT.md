# COG-04G — verified bounded Cartesian scientific operators and fields

Parent: `9f735116db942e98bc7fc8c5e3bacd9fce1c149a` (published E/F candidate; native acceptance separate). Research-only, CPU-first, represented polynomial parameters; no new dispatch, authority, model schema or Lu changes.

## Implemented

- Immutable sparse differential operator in canonical normal form `sum a_alpha(x) D^alpha`: up to three dimensions, derivative order four, at most 35 operator terms, bounded rational coefficients inherited from COG-04F. Addition, scaling, multiplier, mixed derivative, Laplacian and ordered composition. Full generalized Leibniz expansion with independent sequential single-axis product-rule coefficient recurrence. Apply verifies exact sparse coefficient equality against independent falling-factorial monomial differentiation and product accumulation. No numerical sampling used as a proof of these identities.
- Exact scalar and vector polynomial fields on a common closed rational Cartesian box: gradient, Jacobian and Hessian (rows are vector-valued functions), divergence, 2-D signed scalar curl, 3-D curl, componentwise Laplacian, advection `v dot grad`, variable coefficient divergence-form diffusion `div(k grad f)` and Poisson residual `Delta u - source`. Field certificates bind the input snapshots by content identity and replay verified result coefficients. Diffusion independently checked in expanded form `k Delta f + grad(k) dot grad(f)`. Physical units, constitutive validity and boundary conditions are not inferred; conductivity need not be positive for this algebraic operator.
- Continuous residual reports: conservative per-component maximum absolute bounds from exact interval monomials, exact squared L2 integral from rational monomial moments, and exact zero polynomial test. Zero residual is not PDE solution qualification; no initial/boundary condition, target function accuracy or existence/uniqueness guarantee.
- Work, term, order and degree limits fail closed; mismatched domains, dimension, invalid multi-index, atlas/seam derivatives, tampered certificates and deliberately corrupted implementation formulas refused. Domains inherit COG-04F; this is not a general distributional or curvilinear derivative engine. Certificates are mathematical content identities, not authentication/approval.

Existing polynomial KAN worker results at points (1/2,-1/4), (1/4,1/2): gradient [(3/4,1/2),(5/2,11/4)]; Laplacian [6,6]; diffusion [39/4,10]. Three real worker calls; replay exact request/result frames and independent analytical coefficients in gate. No model inference was run. Independent identities: `D_x M_x - M_x D_x = I`, `Delta^2(x^2 y^2)=8`, curl grad and div curl vanish. `Delta u-6` identically zero; `Delta u-(5+x)=1-x` has absolute upper bound 2 and exact squared L2=16/3 on [-1,1]^2, independently checked against SciPy quadrature. No speed or general learned-model superiority claims.
