## L28 constituent methods
- SemiHD confidence-gap pseudo-label iteration (https://graphsearch.epfl.ch/en/publication/7550f32f-5c62-4a63-9ae7-2321becaa049 and https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10462-025-11181-2). Labels withheld from pseudo-training; retrospective only. 5 labels/class; raw logistic self-training control. Full training corpus unlabeled normalization is allowed but disclosed. Need validation-only threshold selection, class imbalance, label noise.
- SearcHD (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/337123755_SearcHD_A_Memory-Centric_Hyperdimensional_Computing_With_Stochastic_Training and https://patents.justia.com/patent/20220019441). KMeans-initialized class-local binary vector quantization and independent stochastic attraction, NOT the original fully binary pipeline/wrong-class update. Packed exact Hamming scoring parity. Need exact paper training/encoder and matched memory.
- TP-HDC (https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14252): independent bipolar task-key bind/bundle and unbinding interference algebra. Two derived class tasks, shared vs independent heads; retained key overhead included, same encoder. Not exact author's learned projection method. Broader tasks and task-count scaling needed.
- MHD (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325860537_Hierarchical_hyperdimensional_computing_for_energy_efficient_classification): independent confidence cascade between two projection encoders, both evaluated in current pilot (no efficiency claim); need original record/n-gram hierarchy and genuine lazy routing.
- HDCluster (https://past.date-conference.com/proceedings-archive/2019/html/0345.html): independent majority binary Hamming clustering vs raw k-means. Known cluster count. No in-memory hardware or original paper fidelity claim.
