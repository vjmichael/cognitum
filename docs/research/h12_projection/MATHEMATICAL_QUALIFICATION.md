## Symbolic mathematical robustness: required operating-envelope gates

Do not equate correct arithmetic with correct associations, similarity with truth, classification accuracy with compositional reasoning, or independent random codes with learned/correlated codebooks. Preserve all 85 targets and their historical evidence; these gates cross-cut existing entries, not 85 newly accepted claims. For each representation specify domain, shape, norm, binding identity/inverse/associativity, ordered versus commutative operations, tie/zero convention, numeric tolerance, and exact/approximate semantics. Exact symbolic facts remain canonical; HDC proposes candidates with provenance, independent witness and explicit abstain/unknown.

1. V01–V16: verify group laws where they hold and construct negative examples where they do not. MAP/XOR commute; nested/ordered relations need role permutations or noncommutative models. FHRR conjugate inverse requires unit magnitude; GHRR ordered adjoint requires unitary matrices. Test composition, inverse order, quantization, norm drift, repeated bindings, and independently computed symbolic truth.
2. E01–E13: characterize pairwise similarity/coherence, Gram spectra, effective rank, collisions, geometry distortion, out-of-distribution transfer and deterministic rematerialization. Match training/calibration/test, dimensions and retained/peak bytes; distinguish code identity from equivalent behavior.
3. V16/M01–M06: derive crosstalk and load/capacity curves at fixed codebook size, dimension and target failure budget; validate across independent seeds and unknown-query controls. Under independently sampled bipolar coordinates, odd K majority of K independently keyed fillers has expected normalized target similarity c_K = binom(K-1,(K-1)/2)/2^(K-1). Derivation: fixing target +1, the rest cancel with that probability, otherwise their signs cancel in expectation. For an independent unknown query and N independent-of-query distractors, Hoeffding and a union bound give P(max score >= t) <= N exp(-D*t^2/2). No independence between the N events is needed; independent coordinates for each target pair are needed. Threshold sqrt(2 log(N/alpha)/D) is a conservative sufficient threshold, not the exact optimal classifier. Correlated/learned/adversarial codes invalidate the naive IID bound; evaluate separately.
4. R01–R09/E06: symbolic role/filler, nested records, analogies, unseen graph topologies, relation direction, path reasoning, compositional generalization, resonator recovery and spurious fixed points. Enumerate small problems with exact symbolic solvers; scale with independently verified witnesses, ambiguous alternatives and unknown fillers. Measure top-k, exact structure recovery, error vs depth/load, convergence/restarts and abstention, not merely similarity.
5. M02–M05/L01–L28: reversible updates, source/version, capacity saturation, noise and drift, class-incremental forgetting, calibration/coverage and selective risk. Evaluate correlated and adversarial cases with held-out calibration and confidence intervals; use exact fallback for high-consequence claims.
6. H01–H12: repeat the matched mathematical checks after packing, quantization, compiler or hardware transformations. Device timing/energy only after actual device-specific reports or measurements.

This is preliminary finite-sample analysis, not a guarantee of unknown-query or target-error rate.
