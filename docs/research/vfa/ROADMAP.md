## Representation and mathematical contract

- Random FPE: real-valued Fourier inverse estimated by equal-weight quadrature on a finite band, random uniform basis by default. Binding corresponds to convolution under that reference convention, not pointwise multiplication. Finite sampling and band truncation introduce separate errors; arbitrary user-supplied basis is not guaranteed uniform.
- Periodic VFA: real trigonometric polynomials f(x)=c0+2 Re sum c[k] exp(ikx), 2pi period; positive modes stored explicitly, conjugate negative modes implied. These are finite spectral vectors, not lossy fixed-D MAP codes. Addition, scaling, shifts, integer affine composition, derivative, zero-mean periodic primitive, finite interval integral, normalized circular convolution and full pointwise products operate on coefficients. Products increase bandwidth; over-budget operations refuse instead of silently aliasing. Parseval energy and triangle sup bounds are representation-dependent. Projection from equispaced samples can alias unknown out-of-band functions.
- Quotient: require sufficient lower bound delta=|g0|-2 sum |gk|>0, with conservative cancellation refusal. Approximate quotient via sampled FFT; verify independently from samples using all residual coefficients r=f-g*q. In real arithmetic, ||f/g-q||inf <= ||r||inf/delta <= (|r0|+2 sum |rk|)/delta. This is not a directed-rounding enclosure: reported float64 estimate can be smaller than measured error at roundoff floor. No guarantee for original functions beyond their represented coefficients. Refuse unresolved denominator or excessive residual; no automatic fallback or division through singularity. Nonlinear composition, nonperiodic products, multidimensional functions, stochastic and certified error propagation are open.
