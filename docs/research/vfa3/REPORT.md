## Tensor-product interpolation and multivariate FPE
For Cartesian grid, K=K_1 kron ... kron K_d. Solve one Cholesky factor per axis by modewise tensor solve, no full Gram allocated in fit. Product condition estimate >1e9 refuses; grid <=256 total points. P_X^2=1-product_j ||L_j^-1 k_j||^2, error <= externally supplied target RKHS norm times power. Dense SciPy distance, full Gram solve and power oracle used only for verification. FPE joint IID Gaussian frequency vectors (one independent frequency per coordinate, inverse length scales) bind coordinate sums by complex multiplication, approximate the product Gaussian kernel; decode values, gradient and Hessian from spectral coefficients, with finite differences validating derivatives of the represented approximation, not target derivatives. Fixed-pair Hoeffding union bound for IID features applies to values only, not derivatives or adaptively selected pairs. D<=65536; no dense D^d feature grid. Float64, no rigorous interval error.
