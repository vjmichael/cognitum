## Scope and mathematical assurance

- Canonical immutable sparse rational coefficients and exponents, one to three input/output dimensions, total degree at most 12, 256 terms, 4096-bit rationals, 4096 tensor-grid identity points per invocation, eight calculus operator steps. Limit overruns fail closed; no expression evaluation or arbitrary external code. Exactness refers to the ideal function of the represented binary64 checkpoint, not a learned target or an arbitrary real source function.
- Extract on a closed rational box from one or two certified Chebyshev or clamped-spline layers. Use conservative exact monomial interval range containment for every intermediate component, including masked coordinates. Select an outer polynomial piece only if containment establishes it; otherwise refuse. Each extracted layer is checked against a separate exact rational recurrence at a complete tensor grid of distinct points with a sufficient degree bound, implying polynomial identity on the certified cell. Spline atlas partitions first-layer knot spans into at most 32 rectangular cells and checks exact common-face polynomial equality. Unresolved later-layer knot crossings are refused, not silently approximated. Atlas cell derivatives describe polynomial extensions; no global derivative/smoothness claim on seams. Operator algebra presently acts on single-box functions.
- Exact addition, subtraction, multiplication and single-divisor polynomial quotient (only with sign-separated denominator and zero remainder), mixed partials, coordinate-anchored integration, ordered operator chains, vector substitution and cross-dimensional composition when domains are certified. Results verified by defining identities on bounded rational tensor grids. Integral identity includes zero value at the anchor. Canonical pinned snapshots; extraction and operation witnesses are replayable and reject alterations; hashes are not authority. Interval overestimation can cause conservative refusals. No general rational-function division, root solving, or nonpolynomial extraction.
- Lossless additive-separable checkpoint export (degree <= 8) and complete bivariate quadratic export on the closed unit box using polarization `xy=((x+y)/2)^2-((x-y)/2)^2`. Binary64 conversion must be exact and the exported checkpoint must be re-extracted to the same symbolic function. More general cross-term export remains out of scope. Newly constructed checkpoints require explicit existing host approval before execution.

## Acceptance experiment

Construct and re-extract `f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2+3xy+x/2+1/4`. Its Hessian is `[[2,3],[3,4]]`, the two mixed partial orders agree exactly, and `D_x f=2x+3y+1/2`. Export the cross-term quadratic as a two-layer KAN and export the derivative as an additive one-layer KAN. Approve each separately in the existing ReferenceTurn, execute at `(1/2,-1/4)` and `(1/4,1/2)`, retain canonical checkpoint, request, result and symbolic bytes. Independent gate replays both actual worker results and checks the analytic values/gradient/Hessian, including mixed terms. Expected values are `1/2,21/16`, gradients `(3/4,1/2),(5/2,11/4)`, and both Hessians `[[2,3],[3,4]]`. Derivative outputs `3/4,5/2`, gradients `(2,3)` and zero Hessians.
