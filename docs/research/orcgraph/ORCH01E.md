**Status: ACTIVE BOUNDED SOURCE CANDIDATE. Native qualification pending.** 01A–D remain independently accepted and unmodified.

## Source and design

Uses 01B exact original-source, declared unit/time/rights metadata, chronological 60/20/20 and publication-availability checks, and 01D original training and calibration outputs. `propose_forecast` requalifies original bytes and independently rederives frozen calibration before selecting; no 01D test predictions or labels enter the choice. `route_exact` reuses the 01A COG-06 deterministic compiler; workers, authority, cancellation and source publication stay with existing MEL/COG-06.

Ridge is the conservative default where affordable. A competitor requires sufficient **calibration-only** MAE improvement by host-set absolute and relative thresholds. If direct ridge cannot fit the evaluation *proxy* budget, a reviewed persistence/train-mean fallback is proposed, or abstention if no baseline fits. Missing or delayed observations, short calibration, inadequate documented units/rights, declared resource proxies and research sample OOD/abstention can reject candidates. The work-unit and model-size figures are deterministic ranking estimates, **not measured wall-clock, RAM, energy or OS enforcement**.

KAN/HDC research combinations require an explicit opt-in and minimum uninterrupted calibration blocks. Even with opt-in they produce only inert research recommendations, never new registered/authorized MEL routes or a certified scientific improvement. A user's override is recorded as unverified intent, and cannot evade data/source or budget eligibility. User authentication and host permission belong to separate real product integration.

`evaluate_frozen_proposal` first recomputes and compares the exact selected decision before touching the held-out test cohort. An altered, stale, abstained, fake approved or calibration-tampered decision is refused. Post-decision scores compare **exactly the selected method** with direct ridge, including negative gains. It never silently changes the winner after seeing heldout data. No independent benchmark, CH⊕aT user-benefit or causal physical claim is made by this layer.
