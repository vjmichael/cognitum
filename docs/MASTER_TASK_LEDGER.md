## 1. Product intent and non-negotiable constraints

- Cognitum is a heterogeneous cooperative reasoning engine augmenting a transformer, delivered eventually as a **separate Lu add-on**. It is not an independent LLM, agent loop, canonical memory store or host authorization service. No Lu source changes authorized by this ledger.
- CPU-first numerical and symbolic execution; GPU resources reserved for the transformer. Evaluate ARM/low-power hardware and optional FPGA kernels when evidence warrants; do not require an FPGA or GPU for baseline functionality.
- **Library-first default (established reasoning portion only):** prefer maintained, appropriately licensed standard libraries with tested CPU execution, bounded native/portable adapters and independently checked semantics. Owner reports that earlier comparative benchmarks favored maintained libraries in the majority of cases, though not universally. This observation guides implementation priorities, not a newly independently audited portfolio-wide statistic. Reuse mature components; do not rewrite conventional solvers for novelty. Owner clarified earlier bespoke implementations were exploratory, not intentional reinvention. Preserve independently verified custom kernels and reference oracles when they provide measured correctness, coverage, efficiency or assurance benefits under matched workloads; choose maintained libraries or custom paths by evidence and maintenance cost rather than origin. Per-component reasoning library review occurs when that component is developed; repository-wide final library consolidation is deferred to COG-02. Owner explicitly excludes HDC, KAN and learned SSM research from this conclusion; their verdicts remain unresolved, especially HDC, under COG-03/04/05. CPU-first remains fixed; prioritize accuracy in high-consequence reasoning, and retain competitive custom implementations where practical accuracy, CPU and memory tradeoffs justify them. No new GPU route.
- HDC (C08), KAN (C09) and SSM (C17-associated research) are first-class independent comprehensive mathematical engines; research routes remain disabled by default. SSM includes classical deterministic/stochastic dynamics, scientific simulation, identification and control as well as learned SSM. Existing admitted C17 reference contracts are preserved. Each deserves a literature-first redevelopment with strong baselines, negative results preserved, and low-resource evaluation; conventional superiority is not grounds to erase a research component. No unsupported claims or automatic promotion.
- Practical C14 uses established causal libraries with explicit assumptions, calibrated uncertainty where validated and abstention. **CH40 pursuit of the global 0.05 gap is suspended indefinitely; no CH40 continuation, certificate search or bespoke solver research under COG-07.** Preserve existing frozen evidence.
- Host-owned ANSF/SQLite remains canonical. Proposals do not grant authority; routing does not execute; preserve scope, source versions, revocation, recovery, cancellation and bounded CPU/RAM/elapsed time. Use the existing typed contracts rather than a parallel registry, journal, worker or host.
- Develop mature reasoning first. Defer repository-wide convergence and final cross-component dependency/architecture reconciliation until the component shape is known. Batch source fixes and acceptance; avoid redundant CI and premature merging.
