There is already a research precedent for exactly these tests
One paper I'd particularly recommend having Qwen use as a benchmark reference is:

A Comparison of Vector Symbolic Architectures — Schlegel, Neubert, and Protzel (2022).

The researchers compared 11 VSA implementations, including tests for:
- How many hypervectors can be bundled before retrieval deteriorates.
- How accurately information can be recovered after binding and unbinding.
- How interference affects recovery when many bound pairs are stored together.
- How representations perform in practical recognition tasks.
In other words, they evaluated the capabilities that distinguish VSA/HDC from ordinary vector retrieval.
